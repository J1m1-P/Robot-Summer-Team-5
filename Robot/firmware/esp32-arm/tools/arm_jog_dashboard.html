Arm Jog Test

Seven-servo and four-stepper test dashboard for src/harnesses/arm_jog_main.cpp. Upload with pio run -e arm_jog -t upload, then connect over USB in Chrome or Edge.

Not connected
Clear the mechanism before connecting. The firmware attaches all servos and initially commands each configured position A. Servo “position” and stepper position are commanded/open-loop estimates, not sensor feedback. A stepper starts at logical 0 wherever it is when the board boots; “Set home” also changes only that logical coordinate. STOP halts step pulses but servos continue holding their angles.

Servos

Position A and B are both targets and the inclusive motion limits. Neutral is their midpoint. PWM edits are temporary and reset to servo_config.h after reboot.

Steppers

Position is signed relative to the chosen logical home. The full range limits the magnitude of each jog in either direction. Defaults come from stepper_config.h: 138, 220, 187, and 152 mm.
