Arm Jog Test

Seven-servo and four-stepper test dashboard for src/harnesses/arm_jog_main.cpp. Upload with pio run -e arm_jog -t upload, then connect over USB in Chrome or Edge.

Not connected
Clear the mechanism before connecting. The firmware attaches all servos and initially commands each configured position A. Servo “position” and stepper position are commanded/open-loop estimates, not sensor feedback. A stepper starts at logical 0 wherever it is when the board boots; “Set home” also changes only that logical coordinate. STOP halts step pulses but servos continue holding their angles.

Automated sequence dimensions

To is an absolute position measured from logical home. By is a relative distance measured from the current position. The operation order is fixed.

Tower pickup sequence
Relative raise from the home position.
Relative lower; match the first raise to return home.
Relative raise after all three tower claws close.
Relative raise after rotating back to vertical.
Tower assembly sequence
Absolute Z expected at the end of pickup, with ±0.5 mm tolerance; no move occurs in step 1.
Absolute Z target; 80 to 50 mm produces a 30 mm downward move.
Absolute Z target after opening the middle claw.
Relative left move from the current Tower X position.
Relative lower after the left X move.
Absolute Z target after opening the right claw.
Relative right move from the current Tower X position.
Relative lower before opening the left claw.
Habitat pickup sequence

Uses the current Habitat Left and Habitat Right servo A angles as open and B angles as close. Tune those angles in the servo cards below.

Relative raise after both habitat claws open.
Relative lower before both habitat claws close.
Relative raise after both habitat claws close.
Habitat placedown sequence

Starts with both claws closed. Position A opens each claw independently at its release point; tune the A angles in the habitat servo cards below.

Absolute starting Z, accepted within ±0.5 mm; Habitat X must start at home.
Relative right move from Habitat X home.
Absolute Z target before opening the left habitat claw.
Absolute Z target before moving across to the right placement.
Relative left move from the current Habitat X position.
Absolute Z target before opening the right habitat claw.
Absolute Z position at the end of the placedown sequence.

Servos

Position A and B are both targets and the inclusive motion limits. Neutral is their midpoint. PWM edits are temporary and reset to servo_config.h after reboot.

Steppers

Position is signed relative to the chosen logical home. The full range limits the magnitude of each jog in either direction. Defaults come from stepper_config.h: 138, 220, 187, and 152 mm.
