MoveS/RotS Calibration Helper

Computes single-speed F_lon, F_lat, and F_ang from three repeated trials per condition, following Tu & Min (2019), "Calibration Method of Mecanum Wheeled Mobile Robot Odometer" (misc/Calibration_Method_of_Mecanum_Wheeled_Mobile_Robot_Odometer.pdf). This simplified procedure is intended for our closed-loop advanced motion controller: the factors provide feed-forward compensation near the chosen operating speed and feedback corrects residual error. Run trials with src/harnesses/calibration_main.cpp ([env:calibration]), physically measure each result, and enter the numbers below. Nothing here talks to the robot -- it is a standalone calculator.

1. Straight-line trials -- one speed

F_lon & F_lat

Command move <L1> 0 <speed> on the calibration harness three times at the representative operating speed below. Before each run, mark the robot's start position and heading (a line off the front). After it stops, tape-measure x_e (longitudinal shortfall from L1) and y_e (lateral drift), and separately protractor-measure θ_e (heading drift from the marked starting heading -- a direct reading, not derived from x_e/y_e). Enter three valid rows. Run this section once for each direction you intend to calibrate (+x, -x, +y, or -y). Record the trial duration because F_lat uses heading-error rate (θ_e / duration), not raw heading angle.

2. In-place rotation trials -- one speed

F_ang

Separate experiment from the straight-line trials above. Command rotate <angle> <max_omega> three times at the representative angular speed in each direction, and protractor-measure the actual rotation against the commanded angle. A 90 degree command is recommended. Run three CW and three CCW trials; F_ang is the mean commanded/actual angle ratio for the selected direction.

Representative angular speed

#θ1 commanded (deg)θ2 actual (deg)

4. Radial error validation (optional)

Once F_lon/F_lat/F_ang are applied, re-run straight-line trials and record the new endpoint error to confirm the correction actually helped -- paper eq. 24-25. "Before" rows can reuse x_e/y_e values from section 1; "after" rows come from a fresh round of trials with the correction factors applied. Not speed-split -- pick whichever speed you care most about validating.
